How the network is linked

ElementDeviceRole on this line
Section gapinsulated gapSplits each track's conductor rail into 4 sections at the substations. Gaps sit at platform ends so a stopped train never bridges them.
Double-end feeding2 × 72 per sectionEvery section is fed by the TPSS at each end, so all five substations run in parallel through the rails and share load.
Track paralleling post72 tie (TPP)Joins the UP and DN conductor rails mid-section to cut voltage drop. Its own di/dt relay trips first on a fault so the healthy track stays live.
Feeder disconnector89 (QS)Visible isolation after each HSCB. Off-load only, interlocked with the HSCB.
Bypass disconnector89 (QB)Bridges the gap at a TPSS when its DC switchgear is out. Closes only when both sides are dead, or both are live with ΔV below 30 V.
Return circuitrunning rails, cross bondsBoth tracks' running rails are cross-bonded and return current to each TPSS negative bus. They are insulated from earth, with VLDs at stations.
Signalling interfacetraction power statusTrains are held before a dead or earthed section and are never driven into it.
Rectifier diodes12-pulse bridgeBlock reverse power. Regen can only go to other trains, a reversible TPSS inverter, or on-board brake resistors.
